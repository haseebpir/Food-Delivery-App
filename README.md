## ✨ Overview

**QuickBite** is a full-scale food delivery app connecting customers, restaurants, and delivery riders in one seamless platform. From browsing a menu to watching the rider arrive on a live map, every step is designed to feel fast and effortless.

---

## 💼 Why Customers (and Clients) Love This

- 🍽️ **Browse Local Restaurants** — Personalized recommendations based on location & taste
- 🛵 **Live Order Tracking** — Real-time rider location on a map, from kitchen to doorstep
- 🛒 **Easy Ordering & Reordering** — Smooth cart flow with saved favorites for one-tap reorders
- 💳 **Secure, Fast Checkout** — Multiple payment options with instant order confirmation
- 🎯 **Personalized Deals** — Offers and discounts tailored to ordering habits
- ⭐ **Ratings & Reviews** — Transparent feedback on restaurants, dishes, and delivery experience
- 🧑‍🍳 **Restaurant Dashboard** — Partners manage menus, pricing, and incoming orders in real time
- 🛵 **Rider App & Route Optimization** — Riders get optimized routes and simple delivery management
- 🔔 **Live Status Notifications** — Order confirmed, prepared, picked up, delivered — every step notified
- 🌍 **Multi-City Ready** — Built to scale from one city to many without a rebuild

---

## 🧱 How It Works

| Feature | Experience |
|---|---|
| **Discover & Order** | Customers browse restaurants and place an order in a few taps |
| **Track Live** | A live map shows exactly where the order is, in real time |
| **Restaurants Fulfill Fast** | Partner restaurants receive and manage orders instantly |
| **Riders Deliver Smart** | Optimized routes get orders delivered quickly and efficiently |
| **Everyone Stays Informed** | Customers, restaurants, and riders get real-time status updates |

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack (Under the Hood)

**Mobile Apps:** Flutter & Dart — single codebase powering customer, restaurant & rider apps
**Backend:** Node.js, PostgreSQL for orders, menus, and real-time delivery data
**Cloud Infrastructure:** AWS for secure, scalable, always-on hosting
**Integrations:** Live maps & GPS tracking, payment gateways, push notifications

This stack keeps the entire ordering-to-delivery journey **fast, reliable, and easy to scale** across multiple cities.
